Founded in 2018

Architecture Expressed in Silk and Cashmere

NF Collections was established in Kaduna with a single uncompromising philosophy: to strip away excess and honor the sculptural purity of garment construction.

Rejecting temporary trend cycles in favor of permanent luxury, each collection is produced in small, limited runs using organic textiles and fine silk.

“Fashion is ephemeral. Architectural precision, impeccable drape, and tactile reverence are eternal.” — Nana Firdausi, Founder & Creative Director
Chrono Lineage

The NF Collections Journey

2018

The Kaduna Atelier Launch

Nana Firdausi launches NF Collections in Kaduna with a capsule collection of 6 bespoke tailored pieces.

2020

Abuja Flagship Showroom

Opening of our main flagship showroom in Maitama, Abuja, introducing private styling appointments for exclusive clientele.

2022

100% Organic Sourcing Charter

NF Collections transitions 100% of fabric supply to certified organic wool, fine silk, and eco-friendly packaging.

2024

Lagos Victoria Island Atelier

Opening of our Victoria Island atelier in Lagos and winning the West African Sustainable Fashion Award.

2026

Global Digital Atelier

Unveiling our high-end digital presence platform, allowing seamless nationwide delivery and worldwide private styling reservations.

Our Guiding Values

The Four Pillars of NF Collections

100% Traceability

Master Artisans

Garments require 60+ hours of hand-patterning, draping, and finishing by veteran tailors.

Zero-Waste Cutting

Advanced mathematical pattern layouts ensure zero fabric wastage during cutting.

Lifetime Care

Every piece includes complimentary lifetime repair and re-finishing services at our boutiques.
